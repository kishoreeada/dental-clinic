## Brand and dependencies

The October 12 identity update uses the original `stackly-logo.svg` and `favicon.svg` from the user's mental-health theme. Their embedded image data, geometry and cropping are unchanged; only their SVG color matrices were adjusted to terracotta (#99543c), with a champagne (#edc8aa) logo variant for the dark footer. These replace the initial dental tooth symbol and recreated wordmark. Manrope, Font Awesome, GSAP, ScrollTrigger and AOS were copied with their existing license materials from the user's previous mental-health project. The previous site's section progression informed the redesign; the dental layouts are implemented separately. Seven additional generated visuals and their exact prompts are documented in `REDESIGN-ASSETS.md`.
