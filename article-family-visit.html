The full journal
01

Check the arrangements

Confirm who has an appointment and when each person should arrive. Ask the clinic what information they need in advance.

02

Share individual needs

Every family member may have different preferences or questions. Let the team know about practical access needs when arranging the visit.

03

Keep the next steps clear

Before leaving, confirm any proposed follow-up separately for each family member. Keep written appointment details somewhere easy to find.

This journal offers general appointment-planning information. Individual clinical questions belong in a conversation with a qualified dental professional.
