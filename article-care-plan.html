The full journal
01

Understand the purpose

Ask the clinician to explain what the proposed treatment is intended to address and why it has been recommended in your situation.

02

Look at the whole plan

Discuss the order of appointments, the estimated cost, alternatives and any follow-up that may be needed. Make sure you know which details are estimates.

03

Take time for questions

If a point is unclear, ask for another explanation or a written summary. Decisions should follow a conversation that you understand.

This journal offers general appointment-planning information. Individual clinical questions belong in a conversation with a qualified dental professional.
