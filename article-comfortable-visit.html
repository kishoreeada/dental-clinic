01

Put your preferences into words

You might prefer a slower explanation, a moment to think or a chance to ask questions before the clinician begins. Sharing preferences helps start that conversation.

02

Talk about the visit

Ask how the appointment is organised and how to request a pause. Agreeing on communication can help you understand what happens next.

03

Keep the dialogue open

Your preferences may change from one visit to another. Let the team know what was helpful and what you would like to discuss next time.

This journal offers general appointment-planning information. Individual clinical questions belong in a conversation with a qualified dental professional.
